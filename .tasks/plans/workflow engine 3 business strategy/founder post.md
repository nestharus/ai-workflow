## My Current Thinking

I have been thinking about what UI/UX means in an AI era for a year. Spaces are where that thinking has landed. Full generative UI is slow and uncontrolled. That's not a product. Widgets and screens are too rigid. Spaces are fluid. They let you control a vibe that people feel while AI organizes and configures for the user.

Picture this. You don't go through a dashboard or menus. You navigate by AI. At the same time there is a way to discover features and get to things you commonly use.

Want to configure your settings? AI

However, you don't just have a chat log. You don't just have a chatbox. We can do an AI-powered searchbox at the top. The AI responds by using the application itself. It opens spaces. It may even open a little chat window to talk to you.

We don't have screens. We have spaces. Areas with a sort of physics to them that define what can be shown and how things interact with each other. That define an experience. We can be in multiple spaces at once. The AI shows you what you are trying to see in each space rather than a static UI.

This is a very new type of UI/UX design. We have a playground. That playground can be configured by AI. Spaces contain spaces. Different spaces have different rules. AI responds with visuals, not text. The designer defines what can exist in a space and how elements interact. The constraints. The physics.
