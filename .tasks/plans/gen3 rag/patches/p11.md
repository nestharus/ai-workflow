The Takeaways
1. The "Sensor" vs. The "Storage"
We do not use the LLM Embedder to store knowledge. We use it as a Sensor.
 * It converts "Text" (which we can't measure) into "Physics" (Vectors b).
 * This allows us to treat Information as a physical quantity (Energy/Tension) rather than a symbolic string.
2. Distillation via Failure (Novelty)
We define "Information" as Prediction Failure.
 * If the Factor Dictionary (D) can predict the vector (r \approx 0), the data provides zero bits of structural information. We discard it.
 * The Graph is built exclusively from the Failures of the Dictionary (r > \tau). This ensures the Graph only grows when it learns something the statistical model couldn't explain.
3. Stronger than Weights
The resulting Graph is stronger than a distilled Neural Net because it is Explicit:
 * Neural Net: Averages "Apple is Fruit" and "Apple is Tech" into a blurry vector.
 * Graph: Detects the Tension (T) between them and creates a Branch or Context Split.
 * It captures the Conflict that the training data tried to hide, turning "Lossy Statistics" into "Lossless Logic".
