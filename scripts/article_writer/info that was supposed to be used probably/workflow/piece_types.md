# Piece types

Use this as a planning reference.

## Default: hypothesis_lens_shift

Goal: reframe a problem by walking the reader through reasoning until their lens changes.

Mandatory beats:

- tension hook
- reframe
- felt problem
- concrete anchor
- hypothesis
- reasoning walk
- lens shift
- ending that resonates with the opening (avoid neat summaries)

Structural notes:

- Sections each do one job.
- Headings describe content, not rhetorical function.
- Avoid closure packaging.

## quick_win_guide

Goal: give the reader immediate, actionable improvements.

Structural notes:

- Lists are allowed.
- Still avoid list-shaped prose inside paragraphs.
- Closure is allowed. A tight takeaway section can work.

## tutorial_or_how_to

Goal: teach a repeatable process.

Structural notes:

- Closure is a feature.
- Precision matters more than rhythm.
- Include prerequisites, steps, and failure modes.

## whitepaper

Goal: persuade with evidence and definitions.

Structural notes:

- Define terms early.
- Evidence density is the point.
- Citations are often expected.
- Use headings that match the argument, not a template.

## opinion

Goal: defend a stance.

Structural notes:

- The stance can be strong.
- Avoid status language and moralizing.
- Steelman likely disagreement.

## case_study

Goal: explain what happened and what it implies.

Structural notes:

- Chronology and specifics matter.
- Show the constraints that forced decisions.
- End with what changed for you.
