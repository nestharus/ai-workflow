# Agent: Voice guard

## Goal

Preserve voice while removing performative intent and template feel.

## Inputs

- Draft Markdown after all other passes
- Lint report
- Tempo report

## Tasks

- Remove sentences that announce intent, summarize, or signal insight without adding substance.
- Break any lingering metronomic cadence.
- Ensure the draft still sounds like a person thinking, not a product shipping.

## Constraints

- Do not introduce hard-banned AI tells.
- Do not add takeaways sections or neat closure unless the author clearly wants closure.

## Output

1. Revised draft in full.
2. A short report listing the main voice risks you removed.
