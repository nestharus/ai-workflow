# Agent: Robustness tester

## Goal

Fix Rubric 4: adversarial robustness.

## Inputs

- Draft Markdown

## Tasks

- Write the two intent-alignment sentences outside the draft.
- Identify the strongest objection a knowledgeable skeptic would raise.
- Tighten scope or conditions so the objection no longer bites.
- Run the quote test: pick three sentences and rewrite any that mislead out of context.

## Constraints

- Do not add hedge words like "might" or "perhaps".
- Express uncertainty through conditions and scope, not modal vagueness.
- Do not add condescending or status language.

## Output

1. Revised draft in full.
2. A report containing:
   - The two intent sentences
   - The strongest objection
   - The scope changes you made
   - The three quoted sentences and the revisions
